*All of a Sudden (2026)* is a masterfully crafted, dialogue-forward film that tackles the ineffable feeling of humanity. 

With a 3.5 hour runtime, it is impossible to find a good time to leave your seat because every word exchanged between the two women are all equally substantial. Their deep curiosity for each others philosophies and minds enriches every moment of their conversation in an indescribable way. 

Their love and interest for each other at first meeting was palpable. The conversation they had in front of the audience after Mari's play was profundly intimate in a way I cannot put into words. Speaking to each other in a language only the two of them could understand was intensely emotional and serene. In that moment, surrounded by all those people, Mari and Marie-Lou were alone together. 

Their encounter answered all the questions they didn't know they had. They both provided something for each other that they didn't know they needed.
