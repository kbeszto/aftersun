Sat too close to the screen and regretted nothing. The sound design does most of the
acting, and I mean that as a compliment — the worms arrive as pressure before they
arrive as images.

What I keep thinking about is how little it wants you to enjoy the hero's ascent. The
crowd cheers, the score swells, and the film is very clear that this is the bad
outcome. Not many blockbusters trust you to hold both of those at once.

Half a star off for the middle hour, which is mostly people explaining prophecy to
each other in rooms.
