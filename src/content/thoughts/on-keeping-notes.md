---
title: Notes are for the version of you that forgot
date: 2026-09-12
summary: A short defense of writing things down badly.
tags: [writing]
---

The useful note is not the well-crafted one. It's the one that exists.

I have a folder of half-sentences from three years ago and it is more valuable to me
than anything I ever polished — because the polished things were written for an
audience, and the fragments were written for me.

> Write like nobody will read it, because mostly nobody will.

That's the whole idea. This site is a folder of fragments with a stylesheet.
