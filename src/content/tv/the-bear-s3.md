The first two seasons earned the panic attacks. This one spends them.

Individual episodes are still better than almost anything else on TV — the one that is
just a kitchen prepping for service in near-silence is extraordinary — but the season
as a whole keeps resetting its characters to where they started. Carmy learns the same
lesson three times.

I'll still watch season four the week it drops.
